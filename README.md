# Privacy Policy - WattShift Power Manager

**Effective Date:** October 17, 2026

**WattShift: Power Manager** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application operates regarding user data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
WattShift is designed as a local personal utility tracker. All electricity bill records, unit inputs, and cost estimates entered into the app are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data or utility metrics on external servers.

## 2. Device Permissions
The app requires minimal or no sensitive permissions to operate. It does not access your contacts, location, media files, or background location data.

## 3. Data Security
Since all data entered into WattShift remains on your local storage, your information is protected by your device's security controls.

## 4. Third-Party Services
WattShift does not share user data with third-party service providers or advertising networks.

## 5. Children's Privacy
WattShift is safe for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this repository with an updated effective date.
